City tsunami evacuation guidance

Redondo Beach Fire Dept: leave the beach, harbor and low-lying areas and head east to higher ground via Herondo St, Beryl St, Torrance Blvd, Knob Hill Ave, Avenue D or Avenue I. If you feel strong or long shaking at the coast, don't wait for an official warning.
